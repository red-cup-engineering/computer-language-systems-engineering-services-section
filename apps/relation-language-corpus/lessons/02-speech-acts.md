# Lesson 2: Speech Acts

In RelConLang, speaking is agency.
This lesson teaches intention, promise, doubt, repair, refusal, capability, and obligation.

## Intending

```text
mi ga tem do nara.
I A intend P intention
"I intend the intention."
```

## Promising

```text
ta tu ga vom do voma.
ACT you A promise P promise
"Actively, you promise the promise."
```

`ta` makes the promise potential: held as a live commitment.

## Doubting

```text
na sa ga dav do luma.
POT 3SG A doubt P uncertainty
"Potentially, they doubt uncertainty."
```

`na` says the claim is held as potential, not fully active.

## Repairing

```text
sha ni ga ren do kelu.
STABLE 3 A repair P relation
"Stably, they repair the relation."
```

`sha` is useful for repair because repair often starts while contradiction is still held rather than already resolved.

## Refusing And Being Able

```text
mi ga nul do koda.
I A refuse P obligation
"I refuse the obligation."
```

```text
tu ga kan do kano.
you A can P capability
"You can / you are capable."
```

## Practice

Translate:

1. I promise the promise.
2. They doubt uncertainty.
3. You refuse the obligation.
4. Actively, they transform the transformation.

Suggested answers:

```text
mi ga vom do voma.
sa ga dav do luma.
tu ga nul do koda.
ta sa ga var do vara.
```

## Saying "That"

Use `ve` when the object of a speech act is a whole clause.

```text
mi ga vom do ve tu ga ran do sava.
I A promise P that you A relate P witness
"I promise that you relate the witness."
```

```text
tu ga nul do ve mi ga kod do koda.
you A refuse P that I A obligate P obligation
"You refuse that I obligate the obligation."
```
