# Lesson 04: Witness, Place, Kind

An agency sentence can carry more than subject and object.
It can say where the claim came from, where it holds, and what kind of reading it asks for.

## Three Particles

```text
pa = from, by witness of, according to
lo = in, at, within the context of
ke = as, as a kind of, read as
```

`pa` comes after the source:

```text
sava pa
from the witness
```

`lo` and `ke` come before their nouns:

```text
lo hena
in coordination

ke vara
as transformation
```

## Sentences

```text
sava pa mi ga mor do tilo.
From the witness, I mark the tile.
```

```text
ni ga lin do kelu lo tilo.
They link the relation in the tile.
```

```text
sava pa mi ga mor do tilo lo hena ke vara.
From the witness, I mark the tile in coordination as transformation.
```

## The Agency Question

Each particle answers a different question:

```text
pa: by what witness?
lo: where does this relation live?
ke: what kind of reading is this?
```

Practice:

```text
From the witness, I relate the relation.
They repair the relation in coordination.
You mark the tile as uncertainty.
```

Answers:

```text
sava pa mi ga ran do kelu.
ni ga ren do kelu lo hena.
tu ga mor do tilo ke luma.
```
