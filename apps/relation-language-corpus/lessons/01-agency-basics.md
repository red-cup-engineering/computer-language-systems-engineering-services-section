# Lesson 1: Agency Basics

RelConLang starts with relations, not isolated facts.

## Pattern

```text
subject ga verb do object.
```

`ga` points left to the acting subject.
`do` points right to the object.

## First Sentences

```text
mi ga ran do kelu.
I A relate P relation
"I relate the relation."
```

```text
tu ga nes do kelu.
you A negate P relation
"You negate the relation."
```

```text
sa ga mor do rama.
3SG A mark P agent
"They mark the agent."
```

## Charge

Put a charge particle at the front when you need to say how the claim is held.

```text
ta mi ga ran do sava.
ACT I A relate P witness
"Actively, I relate the witness."
```

```text
sha ni ga lin do tilo.
STABLE 3 A link P tile
"They link the tile while holding contradiction."
```

## Practice

Translate into RelConLang:

1. I mark the witness.
2. You link the relation.
3. Actively, they negate the relation.
4. Stably, neutral-they link the tile.

Suggested answers:

```text
mi ga mor do sava.

```
