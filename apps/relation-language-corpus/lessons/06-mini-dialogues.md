# Lesson 06: Mini-Dialogues

RelConLang can now practice small conversations.
A conversation is a chain of turns where later turns answer, acknowledge, or repair earlier turns.

## Question And Answer

```text
ku tu ga kan do kano?
Can you enact the capability?

se tu ga kan do kano.
Answer: you can enact the capability.

ha mi ga lin do hena.
Acknowledged: I link coordination.
```

## Request And Uptake

```text
du tu ga ran do kelu.
Please relate the relation.

ha tu ga ran-en do kelu.
Acknowledged: you are relating the relation.
```

## Repair

```text
se mi ga ran do sava.
Answer: I relate the witness.

ku tu ga dav do luma?
Do you doubt uncertainty?

sha mi ga ren do ve mi ga ran do kelu.
Stably, I repair that I relate the relation.

ha tu ga lin do hena.
Acknowledged: you link coordination.
```

## Practice

Write a three-turn dialogue:

```text
1. Ask whether you can relate the witness.
2. Answer that you can relate the witness.
3. Acknowledge by linking coordination.
```

Answer:

```text
ku tu ga ran do sava?
se mi ga ran do sava.
ha tu ga lin do hena.
```
