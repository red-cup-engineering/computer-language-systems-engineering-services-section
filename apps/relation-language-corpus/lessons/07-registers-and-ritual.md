# Lesson 07: Registers And Ritual

The same language can speak in different ways.
RelConLang starts with four registers.

## Everyday

Short and direct:

```text
mi ga ran do kelu.
I relate the relation.
```

## Technical

Explicit and inspectable:

```text
sava pa mi ga ran-en do kelu lo tilo ke vara.
From the witness, I am relating the relation in the tile as transformation.
```

Use technical register when an agent needs provenance, context, kind, and process state to survive handoff.

## Proof

Stable and repair-oriented:

```text
sha mi ga ren do ve mi ga ran do kelu.
Stably, I repair that I relate the relation.
```

Use proof register when contradiction is present and must be held without exploding the discourse.

## Ritual

Parallel and memorable:

```text
ta mi ga ran do sava.
sha mi ga lin do hena.
ha mi ga var-ai do vara.
```

Read it as:

```text
I relate the witness.
I link coordination.
I begin transformation.
```

The repetition is the point.
Ritual speech makes coordination memorable.

## Practice

Turn this everyday line into technical register:

```text
mi ga ran do kelu.
```

Answer:

```text
sava pa mi ga ran-en do kelu lo tilo ke vara.
```

Write a three-line ritual where `mi` does three acts:

```text
ta mi ga ran do sava.
sha mi ga lin do hena.
ha mi ga var-ai do vara.
```
