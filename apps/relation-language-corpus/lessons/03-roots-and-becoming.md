# Lesson 03: Roots And Becoming

RelConLang roots are small families.
A root is not just a sound; it is a memory of relation.

## Root Families

```text
kelu = relation
koda = obligation
kano = capability
```

These three stay near one another.
An obligation is a relation with force.
A capability is a relation with possible action.

```text
voma = promise
vara = transformation
```

A promise changes what future agency can mean, so promise and transformation also stay near one another.

## Beginning, Continuing, Settling, Releasing

RelConLang does not start with English tense.
It starts with process state.

```text
-ai = beginning
-en = ongoing
-um = settled
-o  = released
```

Read these aloud:

```text
ran-ai
begin relating

ran-en
relating in process

ran-um
relation settled

ran-o
relation released
```

## Sentences

```text
mi ga ran-en do kelu.
I am relating the relation.
```

```text
sa ga var-ai do vara.
They begin transforming the transformation.
```

```text
tu ga ren-um do hena.
You settle repair into coordination.
```

```text
ni ga nul-o do koda.
They release the refused obligation.
```
